Exchange Policy

Simple and hassle-free exchange process

Exchange Window: 7 Days

You can exchange any item within 7 days of delivery if the conditions below are met.

Eligibility Criteria

✓

Product Must Be Unused

The item should not have been worn, washed, or altered in any way.

✓

Original Tags Attached

The product's original tags and labels must remain intact and attached.

✓

Original Packaging

The product must be in its original packaging or equivalent safe condition.

✓

Stock Availability

We can only exchange for items currently in stock in your requested size and color.

How to Exchange

Step 1: Contact Us

Send a message on WhatsApp within 7 days of delivery with photos of the unused product and tags.

Step 2: Confirmation

We'll verify your request and confirm the availability of the replacement item you want.

Step 3: Return Pickup

We'll arrange pickup of your original item from your location at no extra cost.

Step 4: Delivery

Once we receive and verify your return, we'll send the new item to you immediately.

What Cannot Be Exchanged

✗ Items with missing or damaged tags

✗ Items that have been worn or washed

✗ Items with visible wear, stains, or damage

✗ Items exchanged after 7 days of delivery

✗ Sale items (clearance/heavily discounted products)

✗ Customized or personalized items

Need to Exchange?

Contact us on WhatsApp to start your exchange process
