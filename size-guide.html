Sizing Tips

• Measure yourself wearing light clothing and not while wearing the item

• Chest/Bust: Measure around the fullest part of your chest with arms relaxed

• Length: Measure from the top of your shoulder to the desired hem length

• Sleeve: Measure from the center back neck to the end of your wrist

• If you're between sizes, we recommend sizing up for a more comfortable fit

• Different fabrics and styles may fit differently - check individual product descriptions
